About Vanguard Urology & Men's Health

A dedicated medical institute founded by Ivy League fellowship urologists to provide world-class clinical precision, total patient confidentiality, and life-changing men's health care.

Our Heritage & Values

Redefining Male Healthcare Through Compassion & Precision

Founded over 25 years ago, Vanguard Urology & Men's Health Clinic was established with a singular mission: to eliminate the stigma, delay, and impersonal experience often associated with men's urological and hormone health.

We have built an environment where men can consult with leading board-certified authorities in complete privacy. From advanced robotic prostate treatments and laser kidney stone ablation to personalized testosterone therapy and vascular ED rejuvenation, our clinical protocols represent the pinnacle of modern medicine.

Outpatient laser and robotic therapies with rapid recovery.

Our Clinical Milestones

Our Mission, Vision & Proven History

A track record of surgical innovation and medical excellence spanning over two decades.

1999

Clinic Inception

Dr. Anthony Rossi founded the institute with a focus on minimally invasive urological surgery in New York City.

2012

Robotic Institute Launch

Introduced da Vinci Xi robotic prostate surgery and high-frequency laser stone lithotripsy programs.

2018

Andrology & TRT Center

Expanded dedicated men's vitality wing under Dr. David Chen, incorporating in-house hormone biomarker labs.

2024–2026

State-of-the-Art Expansion

Upgraded to modern private consultation suites, AI-guided diagnostics, and focused acoustic wave technologies.
