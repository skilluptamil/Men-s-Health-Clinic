Treatment Mechanism

How Rezūm™ Water Vapor Technology Works

Benign Prostatic Hyperplasia (BPH) affects over 50% of men in their 50s and up to 90% of men over 70. When the prostate gland enlarges, it compresses the urethra, leading to frequent waking at night, weak stream, urgency, and incomplete emptying.

Rezūm™ is an innovative, FDA-approved, natural in-office treatment that utilizes convective water vapor (steam) energy. A tiny delivery device introduces sterile water vapor directly into the obstructive prostate tissue for only 9 seconds per treatment site. The steam condenses, releasing thermal energy that gently disrupts the excess cells. Over the following weeks, your body naturally resorbs the treated tissue, reopening the urinary channel permanently.

No Permanent Implants or Foreign Materials

Unlike UroLift clips or stents, Rezūm leaves zero metal or foreign hardware inside your body.

Freedom From Daily Medications

Eliminates the need for alpha-blockers and 5-ARIs which cause chronic fatigue and sexual dysfunction.
