Frequently Asked Questions on Fees & Insurance

Everything you need to know about our billing practices and coverage.

Vanguard operates as an out-of-network boutique practice to provide private, unhurried, and state-of-the-art care without third-party insurance restrictions. However, for patients with PPO insurance (such as Aetna, BlueCross BlueShield, Cigna, and UnitedHealthcare), we provide detailed superbill invoices with all necessary CPT and diagnostic codes. Most PPO patients receive between 60% and 85% reimbursement from their insurers.

Yes. 100% of our clinical consultations, diagnostic blood tests, ultrasound imaging, and therapeutic urological surgeries qualify as legitimate medical expenses under IRS guidelines. You can swipe your HSA or FSA debit card directly at our clinic or online portal.

Your initial consultation includes a comprehensive review of your urological and medical history, physical examination, symptom evaluation, diagnostic assessment, and an actionable, individualized care protocol created by your board-certified specialist.

No long-term lock-in contracts are required. Our TRT program is billed on a flexible month-to-month or quarterly cadence, and you can pause or discontinue treatment with your physician at any time with zero cancellation penalties.

For surgical procedures, our financial team provides a single, all-inclusive bundled rate that includes surgeon fees, state-of-the-art procedure suite access, local anesthesia, consumable surgical supplies, post-op medications, and follow-up checks. There are no additional surprise hospital facility fees.
